💡 How to stay safe in 30 seconds

1. Never share an OTP — no bank, no friend, no “customer care” ever asks for it.  2. Check the domain — google.com is safe, google-login-verify.xyz is not.  3. Hover before you click — short links (bit.ly) hide the real site.  4. Urgency = red flag — “account blocked in 2 hours” is a scam script, not a bank.
